About & Operational Focus

I am a SecOps Specialist and Information Security Analyst based in Bene Ayish, Israel, with four years of operational experience spanning enterprise information security, managed security service providers (MSSP), and 24/7 SOC operations.

In my current role at Overline Ltd., I manage and harden FortiGate firewalls across ~10 managed client environments, lead end-to-end Gurucul NG-SIEM operations (log onboarding, custom parsing, and behavioral detection modeling), investigate alerts across multiple EDR/XDR platforms, and run Wizer phishing simulations. Previously, at Migdal Group, I engineered and maintained over 100 Forcepoint DLP policies, built Splunk (SPL) detections in a 1TB/day log environment, and conducted KQL hunting in Microsoft Azure Sentinel. Earlier at Citadel Cyber Security, I worked in a 24/7 MSSP SOC supporting ~20 clients and served as the designated security account owner for a major Israeli credit-card issuer.

Beyond daily operations, I focus on eliminating repetitive analyst bottlenecks through automation and custom tooling - building n8n threat-intelligence pipelines, client-side browser auditing utilities for Fortinet appliances, home-lab malware sandboxes (CAPEv2 on KVM), and Python automation supporting online child-safety OSINT investigations with Project Ma'at.

Operational Workflow 2026

INCD IOC Enrichment & Enforcement Pipeline

The Problem: Manually checking threat indicators from Israel National Cyber Directorate (INCD) feeds against reputation engines and updating firewall/mail blocklists across multiple appliances takes minutes per indicator and risks false positives.

The Solution: Engineered an automated n8n pipeline that ingests INCD CSV feeds, queries VirusTotal and AbuseIPDB APIs in parallel to filter out benign hashes/IPs, normalizes verified indicators, commits updates via automated pull requests to GitHub, and syncs external blocklists to FortiGate and FortiMail - cutting triage and blocking time from minutes to seconds.

  • n8n
  • VirusTotal API
  • AbuseIPDB API
  • GitHub External Blocklists
  • FortiGate & FortiMail
